Instructions

This extension downloads individual Home Depot order details into separate HTML files.

How to use

  1. Log in to Home Depot and open Purchase History.
  2. Select your desired date range and any Home Depot filters.
  3. Use Table Settings to display columns you intend to filter or organize by.
  4. Click Scan orders to collect orders across all result pages.
  5. Optionally configure Filtering Options to limit which orders are downloaded.
  6. Optionally configure Download Location to select a destination subfolder and organize receipts into subfolders.
  7. Click Test first 2 to test, or Download all to download all matching orders.

Additional information

  • Multiple enabled filters must all match (AND); matching is case-insensitive.
  • The extension shows download progress and reports failures.
  • Settings are retained between sessions.
